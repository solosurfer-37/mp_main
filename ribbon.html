How It Works

Working Principle

High-efficiency mixing powered by dynamic inner and outer ribbon counter-flows.

01

Material Loading

Powders, granules, and processing additives are safely loaded into the processing mixing chamber trough via the specialized top feeding arrangement.

02

Ribbon Mixing Action

The uniquely calibrated inner and outer helical ribbons rotate simultaneously, moving bulk materials in structural opposite directions to achieve exceptionally rapid and uniform mixing.

03

Batch Discharge

Once the desired processing time cycle is safely reached, your mixed material cleanly exits via the automated bottom outlet valve assembly for final packaging operations.

The Ribbon Blender operates on an advanced counter-flow mechanism. The outer ribbon moves material from the sides toward the center, while the inner ribbon moves material from the center toward the sides, creating an intense radial and axial mixing action.

This simultaneous flow ensures that all ingredients are uniformly distributed within minutes, making it highly reliable for even delicate products and complex cross-ingredient batch runs.
