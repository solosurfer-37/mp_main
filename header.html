Our Featured Products

Impact Pulveriser

The MODERN Impact Pulveriser is a versatile and efficient grinding solution designed for fine and ultra-fine size reduction of materials such as spices, minerals, chemicals, and pigments.

Hammer Crusher

The MODERN Hammer Crusher is a rugged crushing machine built for high-capacity reduction of hard and abrasive materials like stones, minerals, and coal.

Vibro Sifter

The MODERN Vibro Sifter is a high-efficiency screening and grading machine designed to separate materials by particle size with precision and speed.

Ribbon Blender

The MODERN Ribbon Blender is a precision mixing machine designed for homogeneous blending of dry powders, granules, and low-viscosity pastes.

Hammer Mill

The MODERN Hammer Mill is a robust and reliable grinding machine designed to deliver consistent and efficient size reduction across a variety of applications—whether it's spices, grains, or industrial minerals.

Screw Conveyor

The MODERN Screw Conveyor is a reliable and efficient material handling system designed to transport bulk solids horizontally or at an inclined angle.
