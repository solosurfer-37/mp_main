Impact Crushing Technology
How It Works

Working Principle

High-speed impact grinding with precision screen filtration and integrated dust control — delivering clean, consistent output every time.

01

Material Feeding

Raw material is fed into the crushing chamber through the inlet, where it comes into contact with the high-speed rotating hammer assembly.

02

Impact Crushing

The rotating hammers strike the material against the crushing liners, breaking larger lumps into smaller particles through repeated impact forces.

03

Controlled Discharge

The crushed material passes through the grate bars or screen openings and is discharged through the outlet once the desired size reduction is achieved.

The Hammer Crusher operates on the principle of high-speed impact crushing, enabling efficient size reduction of bulky and hard materials.

Its robust rotor assembly and wear-resistant crushing chamber ensure high throughput, reliable operation, and consistent output size, making it suitable for a wide range of industrial crushing applications.
